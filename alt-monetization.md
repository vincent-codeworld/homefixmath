## China-resident publisher realities (no sugarcoating)

| Topic | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| **W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E** | Still required on **Impact**, **CJ**, **eBay PN**, and usually **Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy** as non-US payees. This is *lighter than Amazon’s Associates interview + 180-day qualification*, but **not zero paperwork**. Individual → W-8BEN; entity → W-8BEN-E. |
| **Awin tax** | China appears on Awin’s tax-residency list (unsupported countries are those *not* on the list). Complete Tax Details (residency, sole trader vs company, name, local tax ID if any, address). US W-9 is for US pubs; China pubs fill Awin’s non-US tax flow — not the same as Amazon’s US Associates gate. |
| **PayPal** | Common path on Impact, FlexOffers, eBay PN, Gumroad (China mainland: no local bank deposit on Gumroad; **PayPal USD** is the documented path; ~$100 min; PayPal fee applies). Confirm your PayPal can receive **commercial** USD. |
| **Payoneer** | Common for **Awin** cross-border (set up *from inside* Awin; existing Payoneer may not link) and often **CJ** direct-deposit style. |
| **Alipay** | **Do not expect** US retail affiliate networks to pay Alipay directly. Typical chain: network → PayPal/Payoneer → withdraw to CN bank / Alipay *via that wallet’s own rails*. |
| **Chinese personal tax** | Foreign commission/digital income may be taxable in China under your personal situation — Vincent’s responsibility; agent cannot file for you. |
| **Bank / ID** | Vincent still must: identity where asked, tax form, payout wallet, sometimes phone/email OTP. Agent prepares drafts and checklists; cannot legally impersonate him on KYC. |

**Relative friction vs Amazon Associates:** Amazon’s pain was application + tax interview + category restrictions + **180 days / 3 qualifying sales** gate. Impact/CJ/Awin still need tax + brand approval, but **no Amazon-style 3-sale gate** for the network itself (individual *brands* can still reject thin sites).
