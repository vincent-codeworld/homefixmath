How this was calculated

Rows use the dressed face plus the gap as a pitch, then round up. Pieces per row are the run length divided by the stock length, rounded up, without reusing offcuts. Waste is a percent of that raw count, rounded up to whole boards. Example: a 16×12 ft deck, boards parallel to the length, 5.5 in face, 1/8 in gap, 12 ft stock, and 10% waste is 26 rows and 58 boards to buy. This does not size joists or footings.

FAQ

What face width should I use? Use the actual dressed width (often 5.5 in for “6 in” lumber, or the manufacturer’s stated face for composites).

How big a gap? Many wood decks use about 1/8 in; composites follow the brand’s clip or spacer guidance. Change the gap field to match.

Why add waste? End cuts, bad boards, and picture-frame borders eat stock. 10% is a common planning buffer. This count does not reuse offcuts on the next row.

Does this size joists or footings? No — this tool only estimates decking boards, not framing or permits.
