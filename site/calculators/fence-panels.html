How this was calculated

Net length is the run minus the gates. Panels are that length divided by the panel width, rounded up, because a short last bay still needs a whole panel you can trim. A waste percent is applied to that count and rounded up again. Posts are the bays plus one end, plus two per gate, plus any extras you type. Example: 100 ft with one 4 ft gate and 8 ft panels is 12 panels for the layout and 15 posts. At 10% waste the list rounds the panels up to 14. This is a shopping count, not a code or structural check.

FAQ

How many posts for 100 feet? With 8 ft panels and one 4 ft gate, the layout is 12 panels and 15 posts. A 10% waste buffer rounds the panel order up to 14. Posts stay at 15, because spare panels are not extra bays.

6-ft vs 8-ft panels? Wider panels mean fewer posts and faster install, but each panel is heavier.

Do I need concrete? Often yes for wood or vinyl posts. This fence page does not size the hole or count bags. The concrete calculator can estimate a round post hole; confirm depth on site and call 811 before you dig.

Why round up panels? A short last bay still needs a full panel you can trim. Waste is applied after that layout count, then rounded up to a whole panel so the order is not short.
