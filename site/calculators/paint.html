How this was calculated

Wall area is the perimeter times the height, minus doors and windows. Coats and a waste percent come next, then the total is divided by the coverage on the can and rounded up to whole gallons. Example: a 12×12×8 ft room, two doors at 21 ft², two windows at 15 ft², two coats, and 10% waste is about 2 gallons at 350 ft²/gal. Coverage presets are planning numbers. The can label wins.

FAQ

How much paint for a 12×12 room? With 8 ft ceilings, two doors and two windows, two coats usually needs about 2 gallons. Use the scene 12×12, two doors and two windows.

Do I need primer? Yes on new drywall, stains, or large color changes. Buy primer separately from finish paint. The primer checkbox adds that gallon count.

What about trim? Baseboards and door casings usually need a quart or two of trim enamel, not wall gallons.

Why round up gallons? Partial cans still leave you short on the last wall. The page shows the exact gallons before that round-up. Touch-up leftover is useful.
