Estimate disclaimer

The calculators are not a substitute for a contractor, a structural engineer, or your local building code. Always confirm quantities with the supplier, and buy with the store’s return policy in mind.

  • Fence posts, frost depth, and concrete volume depend on soil, climate, and the hole you actually dig. The bag count is a rough allowance (about two 50-lb bags per post), not a specification.
  • Paint coverage changes with color, texture, primer, and how thick you roll. The label on the can wins.
  • Deck board counts ignore joist layout, picture framing, stairs, and railings. Put seams on a joist.
  • Numbers stay in your browser. This site does not upload measurements.
