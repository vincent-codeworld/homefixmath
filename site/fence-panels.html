How we count

  • Counts are for one straight run. If the fence turns a corner, add a post at the corner or calculate each side separately.
  • A bay is one panel width. Posts sit at both ends and between bays, so you need one more post than the number of bays.
  • A checked gate treats one bay as a gate instead of a panel. The gate is assumed to match the panel width.
  • If the length does not divide evenly, the count rounds up to a full panel and the list says how much extra that is.
  • Concrete is an allowance of two 50-lb bags per post, roughly an 8-inch hole about 2 feet deep. It is not a frost-depth specification. See the estimate disclaimer.
