How we count

  • Wall area is the perimeter times height: 2 × (length + width) × height.
  • Each door removes 20 sq ft (about 3 ft × 6 ft 8 in). Each window removes 15 sq ft. Use other openings for anything larger.
  • Coverage defaults to 350 sq ft per gallon per coat. The number on the can wins.
  • The shopping list rounds up to the next quart. Four quarts are shown as a gallon.
  • Primer, closets, and trim are not included. See the estimate disclaimer.
