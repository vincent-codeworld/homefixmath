# HomeFixMath — 5 Calculator Themes (Day 1)

Ranked by **search intent** (DIY quantity queries) × **materials-buy conversion** (Home Depot / Lowe’s / ShareASale-class merchants — **Amazon deferred**). Volumes are approximate US Google Ads / third-party SEO snapshots (2025–2026); treat as directional.

---

## 1. Fence Panel & Post Calculator ⭐ FIRST TO SHIP

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Calculator name** | Fence Panel & Post Calculator |
| **Primary keyword** | fence calculator |
| **Long-tail keywords** | how many fence panels do I need; fence panel calculator; wood fence calculator; fence material calculator; how many fence posts for 100 feet |
| **Why DIY materials convert** | Prefab panels, posts, caps, concrete mix, and gate kits are high-ticket home-center SKUs. A 100-ft run → ~12–17 panels + posts = one shopping trip. Search: “fence calculator” ≈ **9,900**/mo; “fence material calculator” ≈ **1,000**. Strong spring–early summer seasonality — good for HD/Lowe’s / Impact-style links. |
